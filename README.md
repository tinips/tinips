## Featured projects

### Research Outreach Agent

API-based web platform that helps users find potential research collaborators.

The tool retrieves papers from user-defined topics, extracts relevant authors, and generates personalized outreach email drafts to make research collaboration easier and more accessible.

**Main ideas:** APIs, research automation, AI-assisted workflows, author extraction, outreach generation.

---

### UniCat

Full-stack university platform built to improve student life and academic collaboration.

It includes academic resource sharing, event management, and community features for exchange students to connect with other students.

**Stack:** Django, PostgreSQL, Vue.js  
**Focus:** full-stack development, user-facing workflows, student communities.

---

### Geometric Constraint Learning

Research project developed during my AI Research Internship at EPFL.

The project focused on learning structured equality constraints from trajectory demonstrations using deep learning methods, synthetic 3D data generation, projection-based correction, and model evaluation.

**Stack:** Python, PyTorch  
**Focus:** AI research, robotics, geometric reasoning, experimental evaluation.

---

### Multimodal Medical Agents

AI research collaboration with MBZUAI on a project in collaboration with the University of Oxford.

The project focuses on generating reliable clinical information for virtual patients through multimodal medical agents and API-based agentic workflows.

**Focus:** multimodal AI, medical agents, prompt engineering, clinical reasoning, evaluation.
